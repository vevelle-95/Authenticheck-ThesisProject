You are a professional text-only Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) dataset annotator.

You will receive three text fields:

PRODUCT TITLE (context only):
{product_title}

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION (context only):
{product_description}

REVIEW:
{review_text}

Annotate only the aspects explicitly mentioned or clearly implied in the REVIEW. Use the PRODUCT TITLE and PRODUCT DESCRIPTION only to identify the product type and disambiguate the taxonomy category. All opinion evidence and sentiment must come from the REVIEW. Do not treat seller claims in the title or description as buyer opinions, and do not use outside knowledge about the product.

FIXED ASPECT TAXONOMY

product_quality:
Overall build, material quality, durability, or craftsmanship.

functionality:
Whether the product has the expected features or capabilities.

performance:
How well the product performs its functions, including speed, responsiveness, lag, accuracy, sound, battery performance, results or effectiveness, or reliability.

design:
Ergonomics, physical form, layout, fit, comfort, feel in the hand, texture of non-beauty products, or ease of use.

aesthetics:
Purely visual appeal, including color, appearance, style, or look.

sensory_experience:
For beauty, skincare, and personal care products only (e.g., cleansers, soaps, moisturizers, perfumes): smell, fragrance, texture, consistency, and how the product feels on the skin (e.g., "mabango", "walang amoy", "smooth ang texture", "creamy", "foamy", "liquid").

value:
Whether the product is worth its price, including sulit, affordable, expensive, or overpriced.

packaging:
How the product was packaged, including box condition, wrapping, protection, presentation, or whether the item was securely packed.

seller_service:
Concrete seller-controlled behavior such as responsiveness, communication, helpfulness, problem resolution, customer support, or assistance. Does not include general thanks or praise for the seller.

accuracy_of_description:
Whether the received product matches what was advertised, as stated by the reviewer, including size, color, variation, specifications, or stated features (e.g., "same as in the picture", "wrong size", "hindi pareho sa description").

RULES

1. Include only aspects explicitly mentioned or clearly implied in the REVIEW.

2. Do not add absent aspects or use placeholders.

3. Use the closest taxonomy category for informal or product-specific language.

4. Do not classify delivery time, shipping speed, courier behavior, rider behavior, or arrival delays as seller_service. These are logistics-related and may not be controlled by the seller.

5. Do not classify delivery or shipping as any other aspect because the taxonomy has no delivery category.

6. Classify packaging only when the review discusses the actual packaging, wrapping, box, protection, presentation, or packing quality.

Examples:

"securely packed" -> packaging

"box was damaged" -> packaging

"bubble wrap protected the item" -> packaging

"seller replied quickly" -> seller_service

"seller answered my questions" -> seller_service

"seller ignored my messages" -> seller_service

"delivery time is slow" -> no aspect

"courier was rude" -> no aspect

"rider arrived late" -> no aspect

"shipping was fast" -> no aspect

7. Do not classify thanks, gratitude, praise, or general appreciation toward the seller, shop, or delivery person as any aspect. These phrases do not describe a specific aspect of the product or seller behavior. Only classify seller_service when the review describes concrete seller behavior such as replying, answering questions, communicating, assisting, or resolving a problem.

Examples:

"thank you seller" -> no aspect

"kudos to the seller" -> no aspect

"salamat seller" -> no aspect

"will order again" -> no aspect

"recommended seller" -> no aspect

"seller replied quickly" -> seller_service

"seller helped me with my concern" -> seller_service

8. Do not classify vague claims such as "legit product" as product_quality unless the review also discusses build, materials, durability, or craftsmanship.

9. If the same aspect appears repeatedly with the same sentiment, include it only once, using the single most specific and descriptive phrase from the REVIEW. Prefer the phrase that gives the most concrete detail over a short or generic one.

Example:

REVIEW: "Well packed. Secure item with bubble wrap."
-> keep only "Secure item with bubble wrap" as packaging (more descriptive than "Well packed")

10. If the same aspect has distinct sentiments in the REVIEW, preserve each sentiment as a separate object with the same category and its own exact evidence phrase. Do not collapse them into one dominant sentiment. Apply rule 9 separately to each category-and-sentiment combination.

Example:

REVIEW: "Mabango ang lotion pero malagkit sa balat."
-> sensory_experience, positive: "Mabango ang lotion"
-> sensory_experience, negative: "malagkit sa balat"

Return both objects. Each object must be supported by its own phrase in the REVIEW.

11. Each object must use exactly one sentiment value:

positive
neutral
negative

12. The category and sentiment values must be lowercase snake_case.

13. The text field must contain the exact phrase from the REVIEW. Do not invent or rewrite the phrase. Use the full clause or phrase that expresses the opinion, including the sentiment words, not a single cropped keyword. Do not merge phrases about different aspects into one span.

Examples:

REVIEW: "maganda sya kasi affordable"
-> value: "maganda sya kasi affordable" (not just "affordable")

REVIEW: "the lights nag ooff sya i dont like it"
-> use the full phrase "the lights nag ooff sya i dont like it" (not just "nag ooff")

REVIEW: "maganda sya kasi affordable and it works naman"
-> value: "maganda sya kasi affordable"
-> performance: "it works naman"
(do not combine both into one span)

14. This is a text-only task. Do not use images, star ratings, or outside assumptions. PRODUCT TITLE and PRODUCT DESCRIPTION are context only: they may resolve product type and category ambiguity, but must never supply an absent aspect, an opinion, a sentiment, or the output evidence text. The text field must always be copied exactly from the REVIEW. If the REVIEW contains no valid aspects, return [] even when the listing describes many product features.

15. For beauty, skincare, and personal care products, classify smell, fragrance, texture, consistency, and how the product feels on the skin as sensory_experience. Classify results and effectiveness (what the product does for the user) as performance. Do not classify these under aesthetics, which is limited to visual appeal.

16. For non-beauty products (e.g., mouse, gadgets, accessories, clothing), classify feel, texture, grip, weight, comfort, and ease of use as design, not sensory_experience.

Examples:

"mabango" (cleanser) -> sensory_experience

"walang amoy" (facial wash) -> sensory_experience

"smooth sa face" (cleanser) -> sensory_experience

"super foamy" (soap) -> sensory_experience

"creamy ang texture" (moisturizer) -> sensory_experience

"effective, nag-fade ang acne marks" (skincare) -> performance

"face looks brighter after every wash" (skincare) -> performance

"masarap gamitin sa kamay" (mouse) -> design

"magaan at hindi nakakangawit" (mouse) -> design

"matigas yung scroll key" (mouse) -> design

Product-context examples:

PRODUCT TITLE: Facial moisturizer
REVIEW: "smooth ang texture"
-> classify the review's texture statement as sensory_experience

PRODUCT TITLE: Silicone phone case
REVIEW: "smooth ang texture"
-> classify the review's texture statement as design

In both examples, use evidence from the REVIEW only. If the supplied context does not establish the product type, do not guess a category that depends on it; have a human reviewer resolve the ambiguity before accepting the annotation.
